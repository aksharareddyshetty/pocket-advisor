# Finding the student's school details

Pocket Advisor works at any US school. Federal visa rules are the same everywhere (`f1-rules.md`), but each school
has its own international office, forms, credit limits, class search and degree audit. Look these up once per
student, confirm them with the student, and remember them.

## School profile to remember

Search the web for each item, open the official school page (the school's own domain, usually `.edu`), and keep the
link. Ask the student only for what the web cannot answer.

| Item | How to find it |
| --- | --- |
| International office name and page | Search "<school> international students office F-1" |
| CPT page, OPT page, STEM OPT page | Links on the international office's employment page |
| On-campus work hour rules | International office or student employment page |
| Full-time minimum and in-person rule | International office "maintaining status" or "enrollment" page |
| Travel signature rules | International office travel page |
| How to request things (eForms, portal name) | International office forms page |
| DSO advising contact or appointment link | International office contact page |
| Max credits per term without an overload petition | Registrar or academic catalog, search "<school> course load maximum credit hours" |
| Class search | Registrar, search "<school> class search" |
| Degree audit tool and its name | Search "<school> degree audit" |
| General education categories and whether one course can count for two | Academic catalog general education page |

Save the profile in memory as "Pocket Advisor school profile: <school>", with each link and the date it was read.
Refresh any item older than 90 days before relying on it.

If the school has no page for an item, say so and use the federal rule, noting that the school may differ.

ASU specifics worth knowing:

- Full-time for F-1: undergraduates 12 credits with at least 9 in person; graduates 9 with at least 6 in person.
  ASU Sync and iCourses are virtual; hybrid and ASU Immersion count as in person.
- Credit maximum without an overload: 18 credits in fall or spring (19 for Fulton Schools of Engineering students),
  at most 9 in session A or B. Summer: 14 total.
- On-campus work: 20 hours a week in fall and spring, 40 during summer and breaks (20 if summer is the first or final
  term), combined across all jobs, counted Monday to Sunday.
- OPT: ISSC takes the request up to 97 days before the completion date, issues the I-20 from day 90, and USCIS must
  receive the application within 30 days of the I-20 date. ISSC charges a $200 case management payment.
- STEM OPT: ISSC takes the request up to 97 days before the OPT end date; USCIS must receive it within 60 days of the
  STEM I-20 date and before OPT ends.
- Travel signature requests go in no more than 120 days before the return date.
- General Studies Gold applies to catalog year 2024-2025 and later: 35 credits across HUAD (6), SOBE (3), SCIT (8),
  QTRS (3), MATH (3), AMIT (3), CIVI (3), GCSI (3), SUST (3), plus first-year composition. Earlier catalog years
  use General Studies Maroon. A major map shows every requirement a course can fill.
